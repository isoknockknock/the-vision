How to play

1. Choose your formation.
Select how you want your team to line up.

2. Scout three players.
Each round gives you three anonymous candidates, with their current rating, potential, age and value. Pick one or skip the round.

3. Build your XI.
The players are added to your squad. Tap a squad player, then tap a pitch slot to move them.

4. Adjust positions.
You can tap two occupied pitch slots to swap them. The arrows beside the pitch change the midfield mentality when available.

5. Simulate the season.
Once you finish a round, simulate the season to see your league and Champions League results.

6. Reveal the players.
At the end, reveal your final XI and see how your scouting decisions turned out.
