About

The Vision is a scouting and management game where you build your own football club. You scout players, sign them to your team, and then watch how your squad performs over the season.

Each season, you're given a small group of players to scout. Their names remain hidden while you evaluate their position, rating, potential, age, value, and other available information.

You decide who makes the cut, build your squad, and then see how your team performs over the season.

Your scouting record keeps track of the players you've discovered and signed along the way, while your achievements document the story of your club.
